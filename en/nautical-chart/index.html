The chart

Everything on the chart, and a little more

Kummel is built on Traficom's chart data for Åland, the Turku archipelago and the Uusimaa coast, from the Sea of Åland in the west to Pellinge off Porvoo in the east. Zoom in and the details appear: soundings, individual rocks, leading lines and speed limits.

  • Depth areas, depth contours and soundings
  • Shoals, rocks and submerged rocks
  • Fairways with depth and name
  • Navigation marks, lighthouses and light sectors
  • Speed limits and other waterway signs
  • Optional: Traficom's scanned detail charts when you zoom in
Your boat

Shallow-water warning for your boat

Enter the draught and margin under the keel. Water that is too shallow is highlighted, and the depth is recalculated with today's water level from the Finnish Meteorological Institute. Route suggestions go around water that is too shallow, and where that isn't possible no route is drawn.

  • Several boats, choose which one you're driving
  • Route suggestions that avoid land and shoals
  • Routes with arrival time based on the boat's speed
  • Routes and places to and from the chart plotter (GPX)
  • A logbook that records your trips
Aerial photo

See the shore as it really is

Switch to the National Land Survey's aerial photos under Map layers. Fairways, navigation marks and depth areas stay on top, and water too shallow for your boat is coloured red. Tap the water to see the depth there, with today's water level.

  • Aerial photos from the National Land Survey
  • Depth areas and fairways on top
  • Red where the water is too shallow for your boat
  • Depth and water level in one tap
Property boundaries

Where is the boundary?

Turn on property boundaries under Map layers and the National Land Survey's boundaries are drawn when you zoom in, both on the chart and on the aerial photo.

  • Property boundaries from the National Land Survey
  • Visible when zoomed in
  • Works with the chart and the aerial photo
Chart plotter

From Kummel to the plotter on the boat

Plan the routes in Kummel at home and transfer them to the plotter as GPX, via the memory card or the manufacturer's app. Routes, waypoints and tracks from the plotter can also be imported into Kummel, so you have everything in one place.

  • Routes, places and trips as GPX
  • Garmin, Raymarine, Simrad, B&G, Lowrance, Furuno and Navionics
  • Routes are simplified when needed for older plotters
  • Import routes, waypoints and tracks from the plotter
Kummel is not a chart for navigation. The app is an aid for planning and overview and is not certified for navigation. Depths, navigation marks, route suggestions, arrival times and weather may be wrong or out of date. Always use official, up-to-date charts and good seamanship.

Questions about the chart

Is Kummel an official chart?

No. Kummel shows Traficom's open chart data but is not a certified navigation aid. Always use official, up-to-date charts.

Can I transfer routes to the plotter on the boat?

Yes. Under Menu → Chart plotter (GPX) you save routes, places and trips as a GPX file that Garmin, Raymarine, Simrad, B&G, Lowrance, Furuno and Navionics can read. Put the file on the plotter's memory card or share it to the manufacturer's app. It works the other way too: routes, waypoints and tracks from the plotter can be imported into Kummel.

How often is the chart updated?

Traficom's chart data is fetched every week.

Can I see aerial photos?

Yes. Under Map layers you can choose aerial photos from the National Land Survey with fairways, navigation marks and depth areas on top.

Can the route suggestion go over shoals?

Not according to the chart. The suggestion follows the fairways and goes around water too shallow for your boat. If the destination is in an area that is too shallow, the last part is not drawn and Kummel tells you how far is left. Small islets and rocks can be missing from the data, so always keep a lookout.

How does Kummel know if it's too shallow for my boat?

You enter your boat's draught and a margin under the keel. Kummel then highlights water that is too shallow for your boat, taking today's water level into account.

Open the chart

Kummel is free and works directly in the browser. No download and no account. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

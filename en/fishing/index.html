Fishing in the archipelago

One tap when you get a bite.

Kummel's fishing mode shows steep drop-offs, rocks and water temperature on the chart, gives a fishing forecast based on weather and moon, and saves your catch with spot and weather in one tap. Free, on your phone.

The chart

See where the depth changes

In fishing mode, steep edges, places where the depth changes quickly, are marked with orange lines. Together with rocks, shoals and depth contours you see straight away where it's worth a cast or a trolling run.

  • Steep drop-offs highlighted
  • Rocks, shoals and soundings
  • Aerial photo with depth areas on top
  • Save your own spots with a long press
Fishing mode

When is it worth going out?

The fishing forecast combines wind, change in air pressure, moon phase, time of day and water temperature into a score hour by hour. The best hours are marked with a dot, and tapping a bar shows the score for that hour and what speaks for and against it.

  • Score 0–100 with explanation
  • Tap an hour to see why
  • The best time in the next 24 hours
  • Learns from your own catches
Fish!

The catch log that fills itself in

The Fish! button saves the spot, time, weather, air pressure and water temperature. Species, length, weight and photo you add when you have time. The catch can be shared as a smart image, and you decide whether the spot is shown.

Questions about fishing mode

What is the fishing forecast?

A score from 0 to 100 combining wind, change in air pressure, moon phase, time of day and water temperature, hour by hour for the next 24 hours. Once you have logged at least five catches, it learns what usually works for you. It is based on rules of thumb that many anglers use, and is no guarantee of bites.

What do the orange lines mean?

Steep drop-offs: places where the depth changes quickly over a short distance. Fish often hold there, which is why the lines are highlighted in fishing mode.

What is saved when I press Fish!?

Position, time, wind, air pressure and water temperature. Species, length, weight, lure and photo you can add when you have time. You can also move the catch if you pressed in slightly the wrong place.

Can anyone else see my fishing spots?

No, not even us who make Kummel. Kummel has no database and no accounts. Places, catches and photos are stored only on your own phone and are never sent to us. The only thing that leaves the phone is what you choose to share, and when you share a catch as an image you decide whether the spot is shown.

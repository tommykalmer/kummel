The chart from Åland to Porvoo

The whole archipelago in your pocket.

Kummel is a free nautical chart on your phone for Åland, the Turku archipelago and the Uusimaa coast of Finland. Depths, navigation marks and shoals, route suggestions that keep away from the shallows, marine weather with a seven-day forecast, water levels, vessels in real time and a dedicated fishing mode. Open it in the browser and go.

Features

Everything you need before and during the trip

Kummel brings the chart and weather from official sources together in one map, made for the waters we boat in ourselves.

Nautical chart or aerial photo

Traficom's chart with depth contours, soundings, shoals, rocks, fairways, navigation marks and light sectors. Or an aerial photo with fairways, depths and property boundaries.

Route suggestions that avoid the shallows

Choose start and destination to get a route along the fairways, around land and water too shallow for your boat. With distance, arrival time and fuel use. Share the route with a link.

Marine weather on the map

Wind, gusts, rain, wave height and water temperature across the archipelago, now and three days ahead. Wind and rain can be shown as colour fields that move with the weather.

Seven-day forecast

Tap the weather button for the full forecast: wind, air pressure, temperature, rain and water level hour by hour, and an overview of the week.

Water level

Current water level from the nearest tide gauge, with a forecast. The shallow-water warnings take today's water level into account.

Fishing mode

A Fish! button that saves the spot, weather and water temperature. Steep drop-offs, a fishing forecast based on weather and moon, and a catch log with photos.

Offline map

Download the chart in advance, and the map and your position work even where coverage ends.

Anchor alarm

Set the anchor point and radius. Kummel alarms with sound and vibration if the boat drifts.

To and from the chart plotter

Plan routes and save places at home, then transfer them to the boat's plotter as GPX. Routes, waypoints and tracks from the plotter can be imported into Kummel.

Vessels in real time

Ferries and other vessels with name, speed, course and destination, and a photo of the vessel. Search by name and the map shows where the vessel is right now.

Logbook

Tap Record and the trip is saved with distance, time and speed. See it on the map afterwards or save it as GPX.

Your fishing spots are yours

Places, routes, logbook and catches are stored only on your phone. No database, no accounts. Nobody else can see them, not even us.

Aerial photo

Nautical chart or aerial photo, you choose

Switch to the National Land Survey's aerial photos under Map layers. Fairways, navigation marks and depth areas stay on top, and water too shallow for your boat is coloured red. You see both what the shore looks like and where you can go.

Property boundaries

Where is the boundary?

Turn on property boundaries under Map layers and the National Land Survey's boundaries are drawn on the map when you zoom in. Handy when you wonder where the neighbour's plot ends, or how the island you pass is divided.

Boating

Plan the trip in a few seconds

Tap start and destination. Kummel suggests a route along the fairways, and where the fairway ends the suggestion goes around land and water too shallow for your boat. If the rest is too shallow according to the chart, no route is drawn at all, so the map never tempts you over a shoal. Saved places have a Route here button from where you are.

  • Route suggestions along fairways and around shoals
  • Arrival time and fuel use for your boat
  • Warning of gusts above your limit during the trip
  • Share routes with a link or transfer them to the plotter as GPX
Forecast

The whole weather in one tap

The weather button at the top right shows the weather and wind right now. Tap it to open the forecast for where you are, or for the cross on the map. Switch between wind, air pressure, temperature, rain and water level for the next 48 hours, and see the whole week at a glance.

  • Wind and gusts against your boat's limit
  • Air pressure with interpretation: when it falls, the wind often comes
  • Temperature, rain and water level hour by hour
  • Seven days at a glance, with warnings for strong gusts
Marine weather

See the wind where you are going

Wind arrows with gusts and wave height across the archipelago, coloured by your boat's wind limit. Drag the time slider to see how the weather changes over the coming days.

  • Wind and gusts, now and ahead
  • Rain, wave height and water temperature
  • Wind flow in colour with moving lines
  • Exact wind and rain where you place the cross
Vessels

Where is the ferry?

Turn on vessels under Map layers to see ferries, cargo ships and work boats in real time, with speed, course and destination. Wondering where a particular ship is? Search for its name in the menu and the map flies there and shows the vessel with a photo.

  • AIS vessels in real time from Fintraffic
  • Search by name, MMSI or IMO
  • Photo, speed, course and destination
  • How close the vessel comes to you
Fishing

Read the chart like an angler

In fishing mode Kummel highlights what matters for fishing: steep edges where the depth changes quickly, rocks and shoals, water temperature and a fishing forecast that combines weather, air pressure and moon. The Fish! button saves the spot with weather and water temperature in one tap.

  • Steep drop-offs marked on the chart
  • Fishing forecast hour by hour, tap an hour to see why
  • Water temperature across the archipelago
  • Catch log with photos that can be shared as an image
Chart plotter

Plan at home, drive with the plotter

Plan the trip in peace at the computer or on the sofa. When you're done, save routes and places as a GPX file and transfer them to the plotter on the boat. It works the other way too: routes, waypoints and recorded tracks from the plotter can be imported into Kummel.

  1. Plan in Kummel. Routes along fairways, your own places and natural harbours.
  2. Save as GPX. Menu → Chart plotter (GPX). On the phone you send the file straight to the plotter's app, AirDrop or Files.
  3. Import into the plotter. Via the memory card or the manufacturer's app. Ready to go.
GarminRaymarineSimradB&GLowranceFurunoNavionics

GPX is the standard format for routes and waypoints. Routes are simplified to at most 100 points when needed, so that older plotters can handle them too.

Where does the data come from?

Official sources

Depths and chart: Traficom. Fairways, navigation marks and lighthouses: Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency. Rocks, shoals and aerial photos: National Land Survey of Finland. Water level and water temperature: Finnish Meteorological Institute. Vessels (AIS): Digitraffic. Weather forecast: Open-Meteo. Base map: OpenStreetMap.

Kummel is not a chart for navigation. The app is an aid for planning and overview and is not certified for navigation. Depths, navigation marks, route suggestions, arrival times and weather may be wrong or out of date. Always use official, up-to-date charts and good seamanship.
Frequently asked

Questions and answers

Does Kummel cost anything?

No. Kummel is free and needs no account. You open it at www.kummel.app in the browser on your phone, iPad or computer.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Kummel works directly in the browser. On iPhone you can choose Share and then Add to Home Screen, and Kummel opens full screen like a normal app.

Which area does the chart cover?

Åland, the Turku archipelago and the Uusimaa coast: from the Sea of Åland and Eckerö in the west, through the archipelago with Turku, Naantali, Parainen and Kimitoön, along the coast past Hanko, Ekenäs, Inkoo, Espoo and Helsinki to Pellinge off Porvoo in the east.

Can I transfer routes to the plotter on the boat?

Yes. Under Menu → Chart plotter (GPX) you save routes, places and trips as a GPX file that Garmin, Raymarine, Simrad, B&G, Lowrance, Furuno and Navionics can read. Put the file on the plotter's memory card or share it to the manufacturer's app. It works the other way too: routes, waypoints and tracks from the plotter can be imported into Kummel.

Does Kummel work without coverage?

Yes, if you download the offline map in advance under Menu → Offline map. Then the chart, your position and your saved places and routes work even out in the archipelago where coverage is poor. Weather and water level need a connection.

Where are my places and catches stored?

Only on your own phone or computer. Nothing is sent to us. You can move everything between phone and computer with a backup.

How long are my routes and catches kept?

Until you delete them yourself. Add Kummel to your home screen and it counts as an app of its own, so the browser won't clear anything. Under Menu → Move and back up you save a copy of everything as a file, and Kummel gently reminds you when a lot of new things have piled up since last time.

Can the route suggestion go over land or shoals?

The route suggestion keeps to the fairways and to water deep enough for your boat according to the chart. Where the chart shows water that is too shallow, Kummel draws no route at all, and tells you how far is left and that you need to navigate yourself. Small islets and rocks can be missing from the data, so always keep a lookout.

Can I get the buttons off the map?

Yes. Tap the small arrow under the menu button and almost all buttons are hidden, giving the map the whole screen. Speed, course, position and water level are always shown. Another tap brings the buttons back.

Found an error in the map, or have an idea?

Email kontakt@kummel.app. Tell us where the error is. We read everything and report errors in the chart to the right authority.

Can I navigate with Kummel?

No. Kummel is an aid for planning and overview, not a certified navigation system. Always use official charts and good seamanship.

Water level

How much water is there right now?

Kummel shows the water level from the nearest tide gauge, how it has changed over the last day, and the forecast ahead. Chart depths refer to mean sea level, and Kummel recalculates the shallow-water warnings for today's level.

  • Measured water level and trend
  • Forecast with lowest and highest level
  • Shallow-water warnings that include the water level
  • "Right now about X m" when you tap the water
Marine weather

Wind, rain and waves across the archipelago

The wind arrows show mean wind and gusts, coloured by your boat's wind limit. Choose Flow to see the wind as a colour field with moving lines, or Rain to see where and when it rains. Water° shows the water temperature.

  • Wind and gusts in m/s
  • Rain in mm per hour
  • Wave height and water temperature
  • Exact values where you place the cross
Forecast

The weather where you're going

Tap the weather button at the top right. The forecast applies to where you are, or to the cross on the map. Switch between wind, air pressure, temperature, rain and water level, and see the whole week in an overview where days with strong gusts for your boat show in red.

  • Wind and gusts 48 hours ahead
  • Air pressure with interpretation
  • Temperature and rain hour by hour
  • Water level with forecast
  • Seven days at a glance

Questions about water level and weather

Where does the water level come from?

From the Finnish Meteorological Institute's tide gauges. Kummel uses the nearest of Degerby (Föglö), Ruissalo (Turku), Hanko, Helsinki, Porvoo and Rauma, and also shows the forecast for the coming days.

What does +12 cm mean?

That the water is 12 cm above mean sea level. Chart depths refer to mean sea level, so it is then about 12 cm deeper than the chart shows. With a minus it is shallower.

Does Kummel take the water level into account?

Yes. The shallow-water warnings and the depth box take today's water level into account. You can switch it off in the water level panel if you prefer to go by the chart depths.

How far ahead does the marine weather go?

The map shows wind, gusts, rain, wave height and water temperature now and three days ahead. Drag the time slider to see how it changes. The weather button at the top right opens the forecast for a place: 48 hours hour by hour and seven days as an overview.

Does Kummel show air pressure?

Yes. The weather forecast includes air pressure for the next 48 hours, with an interpretation of how it is changing. A clearly falling pressure often means more wind and worse weather on the way.
